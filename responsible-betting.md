# Responsible Betting

Bet Latino AI promotes responsible participation.

Sports betting involves financial risk, including the possibility of losing the entire amount wagered.

Users should:

- Participate only where legally permitted.
- Meet applicable legal age requirements.
- Use a predetermined entertainment budget.
- Never borrow money to gamble.
- Never use money intended for rent, groceries, utilities or transportation.
- Avoid chasing losses.
- Review sportsbook terms and conditions.
- Stop if betting creates financial or emotional harm.

Bet Latino AI does not guarantee profits or financial results.
