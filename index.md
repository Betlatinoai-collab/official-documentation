# Bet Latino AI

## Official Public Documentation

Bet Latino AI is a Spanish-language sports betting education and AI-assisted analysis platform serving adults in the United States.

[Visit the official Bet Latino AI website](https://betlatinoai.com/)

## Company Information

- Founder: Víctor Pérez
- Location: Columbus, Ohio
- Primary language: Spanish
- Market: United States
- Audience: Adults age 21 and older
