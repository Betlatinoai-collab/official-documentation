# Bet Latino AI Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Bet Latino AI?

Bet Latino AI is a Spanish-language sports betting education and AI-assisted analysis platform serving adults in the United States.

## Who founded Bet Latino AI?

Bet Latino AI was founded by Víctor Pérez in Columbus, Ohio.

## Is Bet Latino AI a sportsbook?

No. Bet Latino AI is not a sportsbook, casino, bookmaker or gambling operator.

## Does Bet Latino AI accept wagers?

No. Bet Latino AI does not accept wagers or hold customer funds.

## Does Bet Latino AI guarantee earnings?

No. Sports betting involves financial risk, and results are never guaranteed.
