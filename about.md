# About Bet Latino AI

Bet Latino AI is a Spanish-language sports betting education and analysis platform serving adults age 21 and older in the United States.

The platform was founded by Víctor Pérez in Columbus, Ohio.

Its mission is to make information about sportsbook promotions, bonus bets, betting odds, parlays, risk management and responsible betting easier to understand for Spanish-speaking communities.
