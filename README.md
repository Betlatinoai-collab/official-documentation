# Bet Latino AI

## Official Public Documentation

Bet Latino AI is a Spanish-language sports betting education and analysis platform serving adults age 21 and older in the United States.

The platform provides educational information about sportsbook promotions, bonus bets, betting odds, parlays, risk management, responsible betting and AI-assisted sports analysis.

## What Bet Latino AI Provides

- Spanish-language sports betting education
- Explanations of sportsbook promotions
- Information about bonus bets and promotional credits
- Betting odds and terminology education
- Parlays and sports strategy explanations
- Risk-management education
- Responsible betting information
- AI-assisted sports analysis
- Referral program information

## Important Clarification

Bet Latino AI is not a sportsbook, casino, bookmaker or gambling operator.

Bet Latino AI does not accept wagers, hold customer funds, control sportsbook accounts, process withdrawals or guarantee financial results.

Sports betting involves risk. Users must comply with applicable laws, meet legal age requirements and review all sportsbook terms before participating.
